Carrots are crunchy, sweet root vegetables packed with beta-carotene, fiber, and Vitamin A. Best known for supporting eye health and immunity, they are delicious raw or cooked in soups, roasts, and salads.

Corn is a sweet and versatile grain rich in carbohydrates, dietary fiber, and B vitamins. Enjoy it fresh, roasted, or cooked into soups and salads.

Bell peppers are vibrant, crunchy vegetables packed with Vitamin C, fiber, and antioxidants. They are delicious raw in salads, stir-fried, or roasted.
